Refrigerator Repair Service Certified Technicians

Your refrigerator runs around the clock, so even a minor fault can affect your food and daily routine. From cooling problems to unusual noises, our technicians identify the issue quickly and repair it with care for long-lasting performance.

Common Problems We Fix

Not Cooling Properly

A faulty compressor, low refrigerant or blocked vents can reduce cooling efficiency.

Water Leakage

Blocked drain pipes or a damaged door seal are common causes of leaks.

Unusual Noises

Buzzing, clicking or grinding sounds may point to compressor or fan motor issues.

Ice Buildup in Freezer

A faulty defrost system or worn door seal can cause excess ice buildup.

Compressor Not Starting

Electrical faults or a worn-out relay can prevent the compressor from starting.
