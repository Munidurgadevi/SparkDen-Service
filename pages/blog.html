QUICK TIP

Pay Attention to the Small Warning Signs

Appliances often give small indications before a problem becomes more noticeable. Unusual sounds, burning smells, overheating, inconsistent performance or damaged wires should be taken seriously.

If an appliance appears unsafe, switch it off and seek appropriate professional help instead of continuing to use it.
